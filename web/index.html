How to play

elpoop is the reverse of Poople. Every day starts with POOP.

  1. Change exactly one letter to make a new valid 4-letter word.
  2. Keep going, one letter at a time, forming a ladder.
  3. Reach the day's target word in as few steps as possible.

Example: POOP → LOOP → LOOT → LOOK

The white row at the bottom of the board is your target. No adding, removing, or rearranging letters — and every rung must be a real word. Tiles turn when a letter matches the target's letter in that spot.

Cornered? Undo step backs you up. One puzzle per day.

How to play Elpoop

Elpoop is the reverse of Poople. Every day starts the same way: the word POOP.

  1. Change exactly one letter to make a new valid 4-letter word.
  2. Keep changing one letter at a time, word after word, forming a ladder.
  3. Reach today's target word in as few steps as possible.

Example: POOP → LOOP → LOOT → LOOK

Every word in your ladder must be a real word. Each step may only change one letter in place — no adding, removing, or rearranging letters.

Cornered yourself? Use Undo step to back up and try a different branch.

Your board tiles turn when that letter already matches the target word's letter in that position. The letter you just changed gets a outline.

One puzzle per day — come back tomorrow for a new target!
